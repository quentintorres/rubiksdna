01 / Research Programs

Every rotation is a reversible move.

We treat the aging epigenome as a scrambled cube: high-dimensional, but governed by a finite set of reversible operations. Our four programs each attack one face.

◩

Epigenetic Reprogramming

Partial reprogramming with transient OSK expression to reset methylation clocks without erasing cell identity.

PROGRAM RX-01
◪

Senolytic Discovery

Machine-guided screens that identify compounds clearing senescent cells while sparing healthy tissue.

PROGRAM SN-02
◨

Genomic Stability

Engineering enhanced DNA damage response pathways to slow the accumulation of somatic mutations.

PROGRAM GS-03
◧

Combinatorial Clocks

Next-generation aging biomarkers built on group theory — measuring distance-to-solved, not just elapsed time.

02 / The Platform

CUBESOLVER™

Our computational engine searches the space of epigenetic interventions the way a speedcuber searches move sequences — pruning trillions of paths to find the shortest route back to youth.

  • SCAN Single-cell multi-omic profiling captures the scrambled state.
  • SOLVE Graph search over intervention space finds minimal reprogramming sequences.
  • ROTATE Targeted delivery executes the moves — in vitro, then in vivo.
  • VERIFY Combinatorial clocks confirm distance-to-solved has decreased.
