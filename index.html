We're pursuing the same idea for the genome's regulatory state: deliberate, reversible moves toward health, not brute-force edits. The cube math is real; the biology is an open problem we're honest about.

01 / What We're Exploring

Every rotation is a reversible move.

The metaphor: treat the aging epigenome as a scrambled cube — high-dimensional, but navigable by reversible operations. These are the areas we study. None of them are solved, by us or anyone; the real literature behind each is below.

◩

Epigenetic Reprogramming

Partial reprogramming with transient factor expression — an active field since Ocampo (2016) and Lu (2020) — and its open safety questions.

Senescent Cell Clearance

Genomic Stability

How DNA damage response pathways shape the accumulation of somatic mutations — and whether they can be safely enhanced.

AREA GS-03

Aging Clocks

Methylation clocks in the tradition of Horvath (2013) — and our open question: can "distance to healthy" be measured like distance to solved?

02 / The Platform — In Development

CUBESOLVER

The engine we're building: search the space of epigenetic interventions the way a solver searches move sequences. This is the design, not a result — nothing here has been validated yet, and we'll say so plainly when it is.

  • SCAN Profile the epigenetic state from single-cell data.
  • SOLVE Search intervention space for minimal reprogramming sequences.
  • ROTATE Test candidate sequences — in silico first, then in vitro.
  • VERIFY Measure whether validated aging clocks actually moved.
